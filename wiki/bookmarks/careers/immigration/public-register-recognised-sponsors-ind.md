# Public Register Recognised Sponsors | IND

https://ind.nl/en/public-register-recognised-sponsors

## Summary

A recognised sponsor is a company, school, or organisation that has been recognised by the IND. A recognised sponsor can apply for a residence permit for, for example, employees, au pairs or students. Find out which organisations are recognised sponsors per category.
