# Almost all verbs with -ij- are strong. If a verb has -ij-, it almost always turn

Almost all verbs with -ij- are strong. If a verb has -ij-, it almost always turns into -ee- in both the simple past and the participle:
kijken - keek - gekeken (to look)
bijten - beet - gebeten (to bite)

If a verb is strong and has -i- or -e-, it will almost always have -o- in both the past and the participle:
zwemmen - zwom - gezwommen (to swim)
zingen - zong - gezongen (to sing)
klimmen - klom - geklommen (to climb)

If a verb is strong and has -ui-, it will always have -oo- in the past and past participle:
duiken - dook - gedoken (to dive)
spuiten - spoot - gespoten (to spout)
Common exception: gebruiken - gebruikte - gebruikt (to use)

If a verb is strong has has -ee-, it will almost always have -a- in the past and -o- in the participle:
stelen - stal - gestolen (to steal)
nemen - nam - genomen (to take)
Common exception: lezen - las - gelezen (to read)
