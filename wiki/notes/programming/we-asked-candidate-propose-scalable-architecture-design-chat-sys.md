# We asked the candidate to propose a scalable architecture design for a Chat Syst

We asked the candidate to propose a scalable architecture design for a Chat System. The candidate have asked initially how many users and chat rooms do we anticipate. And yet his initial design was not scalable at all and could not handle the suggested load. We had to ask many hinting questions to drive the solution to something scalable. After 1 hour discussion we still did not get good enough scalable system design and we switched to some theoretical questions about horizontal scalability, design patters for distributed systems, etc. While the candidate showed some knowledge of these topics his answers ware not deep enough to assess whether he understands horizontal scalability, Saga design pattern, etc. Overall the candidate did not show good experience in designing scalable distributed software systems.
