Pro Tip

The #1 reason candidates get caught — they sound like they’re reading

Other AI tools give you polished paragraphs. The problem? Nobody speaks in perfect prose. When you read a wall of text out loud, interviewers hear it instantly — the pacing is off, the words are too formal, and you sound like you’re reciting, not thinking.

That’s why we designed the Glance-First Format — based on thousands of analyzed interviews and real user feedback. Every answer starts with a natural opening line you say immediately, followed by short bullet points you riff on in your own words. You glance for 2 seconds, then talk — like checking your notes, not reading a script.
