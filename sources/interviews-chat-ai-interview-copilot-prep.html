Frequently asked questions

If you can’t find what you’re looking for, email our support team.

    • Is my meeting app supported?

      Interview Copilot works with any virtual meeting platform, including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and more. The web version requires no installation, though you can optionally install our Chrome extension for added convenience. Desktop meeting apps are also supported via the Copilot Desktop app. For phone interviews, simply use the web version of Copilot on a separate device.

    • Is my data secure with Interviews Chat?

      Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption and make every effort to minimize the amount of data we collect. We do not store any audio or video. They are processed in real time and exist only for the duration of the browser session. For more information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

    • Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

      Yes, you can cancel anytime through Manage Subscription button on the Account page in the app, and you'll retain full access until the end of your billing period.

    • Are AI suggestions always accurate?

      We use advanced Large Language Models from top AI research companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. You can test each one and even switch between models during your interview. While these models are constantly improving, they may not always interpret every situation perfectly, so always trust your own judgment. Interview Copilot is here to keep you on track, but it’s not a replacement for your own knowledge and experience.

    • Can the interviewer detect if I'm using Copilot during the interview?

      No, our Copilot doesn’t join the meeting or notify anyone. It simply monitors the conversation quietly in the background. To keep your delivery natural, avoid reading AI suggestions word for word. Instead, use them as a guide to craft responses in your own voice. The bulleted list suggestion format is especially useful for quick reference. Suggestions appear near the center of your screen, but try to maintain eye contact with the camera to avoid appearing like you're reading.

    • Does Interviews Chat support languages other than English?

      Yes, our platform supports multiple languages. You can switch the live transcription language at any time during the interview. By default, it matches the app's selected language. The chosen language also controls the language of all AI responses.

    • Why should I use Interview Copilot even if I'm well-prepared?

      Interviewers often ask questions designed to throw you off, diving into areas you haven't revisited in months, or even years, because they’re not part of your everyday tasks. Even the most prepared candidates can struggle to recall key details in these moments. To make things trickier, many employers expect STAR responses for behavioral questions, which often feels unnatural compared to how we typically communicate. Copilot is built to help you navigate these hurdles with real-time suggestions, keeping you sharp, focused, and ready to leave a lasting impression.
