# Content ownership (planning artifacts)

Four layers — **proposal → specs → design → tasks** — each adds **delta only**. Canonical matrix: [planning-artifact-loop.md](../planning-artifact-loop.md) ## Content ownership. Do not restate upstream content when writing or reviewing artifacts; cite Requirement titles and Decision N downstream. **tacos-spec-review** enforces cross-artifact distinctness (overlap → MAJOR); **tacos-apply-review** layered compliance treats thin proposals and design/tasks deferrals as expected — not spec drift.
